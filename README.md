### Hi, I'm Ian! 👋

I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Physics at Caltech, where my research focuses on the development of precision control systems for gravitational physics experiments. My work at the Laser Interferometer Gravitational-Wave Observatory (LIGO) has centered on designing and implementing optimal control algorithms to push the limits of instrument sensitivity.

My projects often involve a blend of software, hardware, and deep physical principles, and I am passionate about taking complex systems from a theoretical concept to a fully deployed and functioning instrument.

---

#### 🛠️ My Expertise

* **Optimal Controls:** My core expertise lies in designing optimal $\\mathcal{H}\_{\\infty}$ bounded LQG control algorithms and state-space modeling. I have hands-on experience with Riccati/Lyapunov approaches and traditional PID controllers, applying them to real-world systems to reduce noise and enhance stability.
* **Optics & Quantum Sensing:** I have extensive experience building in-vacuum, high-finesse optical cavities, developing laser frequency stabilization systems, and modeling coupled-cavity systems for quantum noise isolation.
* **Software Development:** My go-to languages are Python and MATLAB for research, data analysis, and system control. I also have skills in Java, SQL, and Bash.
* **Machine Learning:** I developed a novel Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) agent using Python, SQL, and custom vector databases to streamline knowledge retrieval across the LIGO organization.
* **Hardware:** I have practical experience with FPGAs, RF electronics, signal analyzers, additive manufacturing, circuit board design and manufacturing, and most modern optics hardware.

---

#### 💡 Featured Projects

* **Buzz Control:** I developed and implemented a suite of optimal $\\mathcal{H}\_{\\infty}$ bounded LQG control algorithms for LIGO, which enhanced interferometric sensitivity by orders of magnitude.
* **LIGO Labrador:** I led the creation of an organization-wide RAG AI system to provide quick access to technical knowledge. This project leverages Python, SQL, and custom vector databases. Learn more at [ligolabrador.com](https://ligolabrador.com/)
* **OSELOTS Spectrograph:** As a Visiting Research Fellow at Harvard, I developed the Overall Sky Emission Line Observing and Tracking Spectrograph (OSELOTS), a rugged, field-deployed instrument for the Vera Rubin Observatory.
